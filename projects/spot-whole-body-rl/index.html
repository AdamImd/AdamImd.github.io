About these rollouts

Native HD studio replays of recorded simulator states from separate reinforcement-learning studies. The saved policies are frozen during state capture; the studio replays their motion kinematically. Box-tipping footage comes from a partial policy checkpoint. The crouch policy learns leg-joint control with the arm stowed; its full assessment did not meet the study's criterion in every tested condition. Selected behaviors do not estimate overall success or demonstrate physical-robot performance.

Simulation and rendering: NVIDIA Isaac Lab. Spot model and configuration: ReLIC.
